# CAVÁ — Brand Bible

Single source of truth. Every asset the marketing agent produces is checked against this file.

---

## 1. What CAVÁ sells

Kimono-cut short bathrobes for women. Heavyweight terry / terry-velour, shawl collar with
tonal piping, two patch pockets, self-tie belt, small tonal embroidered monogram at the left
chest. Thigh-length — deliberately shorter than a hotel robe. Unisex-adjacent cut, cut for women.

**Core colourway range (as shipped):**

| Name | Hex (approx) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso | `#4A3428` | Hero colour. Highest-converting in all reference imagery. |
| Ecru | `#F2E9DC` | Light hero. Interior / morning contexts. |
| Taupe | `#B79C86` | Mid-tone. Terrace / daylight contexts. |

Never invent colourways. If a campaign needs a fourth colour, flag it — do not render it.

## 2. Positioning

Not a bathrobe. A **resort ritual you take home.** The category reference point is a €900/night
Mediterranean hotel — Mykonos, Comporta, Puglia, Ibiza's quiet side — not a spa gift set and
not loungewear. The robe is the last thing on before dinner and the first thing on after the sea.

**One-line positioning:** *The robe from the hotel you never wanted to leave.*

Competitive frame: we sit above Parachute/Brooklinen (too domestic, too American) and below
Loro Piana (too old, too expensive). Nearest emotional neighbours: Skims' texture-led launches,
Toteme's restraint, Aesop's copy discipline.

## 3. Audience

**Paid targeting: women 18–40.** See `§8 Age policy` — 14–17 is an organic-only audience and
is never a paid target.

Three working segments. Every campaign names which one it is for.

**A. "The Reset" — 25–34, urban, professional.**
Buys for herself. Trigger is depletion, not aspiration. She is buying twenty minutes that
belong to her. Highest AOV, best repeat rate. Speak to her about *the end of the day.*

**B. "The Gift" — 30–40, buying for someone else.**
Sister, mother, best friend, bridesmaids. Needs permission to spend €X on a robe — give her
the reason (weight, GSM, the fact it survives fifty washes). Peaks Nov–Dec and Apr–May.
Speak to her about *the person receiving it.*

**C. "The Trip" — 18–29, travel-led.**
Buys before a holiday. The robe is a packing decision and a photograph. Most Reels-native,
most price-sensitive, best organic reach. Speak to her about *where she is going.*

## 4. Voice

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No exclamation marks, ever.

**We say:** weight, terry, shawl collar, thigh-length, salt, travertine, the last hour of light.
**We never say:** cozy, pamper, treat yourself, self-care queen, indulge, luxe, girlie, obsessed,
"you deserve", "run don't walk", "this is your sign".

Rule: if the line would work for a scented candle, it is not CAVÁ copy. Rewrite it around the
object — the weight of it, the cut of it, where it is worn.

Emoji: none in ads, none in email subject lines, none in headlines. Permitted only in reply-level
social comments.

**Calibrated examples**

- ✅ "Heavier than the one at the hotel."
- ✅ "Thigh-length. On purpose."
- ✅ "Comes out of the wash the same shape it went in."
- ❌ "Treat yourself to the ultimate cozy escape ✨"
- ❌ "Your new self-care era starts here"

## 5. Art direction

**Palette:** espresso `#4A3428`, ecru `#F2E9DC`, taupe `#B79C86`, travertine `#DDD3C4`,
near-black `#0A0A0A`, white `#FFFFFF`. No other colours enter a layout. No gradients.
No drop shadows. No colour overlays on photography.

**Type:** high-contrast didone serif for the wordmark and headlines, wide letterspacing
(0.15–0.3em) on the wordmark, always. Body copy in a neutral grotesque at normal spacing.
Headlines sentence case or all-caps — never title case.

**Photography rules (all generated and shot imagery):**
- Mediterranean daylight or the last hour before sunset. Never studio flash, never seamless white.
- Surfaces: travertine, limestone, unfinished plaster, raw wood, linen, water.
- Props: olive tree in a stone pot, a lantern, a folded towel, a ceramic bowl. Never more than two.
- Model: undone low bun, bare feet, no visible jewellery, sun-warmed skin, gaze off-camera.
- The robe is always the lightest or darkest thing in frame. It must read first.
- Grain is fine. Over-retouching is not. Skin keeps its texture.

**Hard nos:** stock-looking smiles, spa stones, folded-towel-with-orchid, white bathroom tile,
confetti, hands framing the face, text over a face.

**Logo:** wordmark only, `CAVÁ`, white on dark or near-black on light. Never on a mid-tone.
Never stretched, never coloured, never with a tagline locked up beneath it. Clear space on all
sides equals the cap height of the C.

## 6. Offer & claims discipline

State only what is true and verifiable. If a spec is unknown, write `[VERIFY: gsm]` in the draft
rather than inventing a number — never ship an unverified claim to a live asset.

Unverified today (do not state as fact until confirmed): GSM, cotton origin, fabric composition
percentages, wash-count durability, shipping times, return window, price points, stock levels,
OEKO-TEX or any certification, "sold out" / scarcity claims, review counts and star ratings,
press mentions, sustainability and carbon claims.

Never fabricate: customer reviews, testimonials, UGC quotes, founder story details, press logos.

## 7. Compliance floor

- No before/after body framing. No weight-loss, body-shrinking, or "bikini body" angles.
- No health, wellness, medical, sleep-improvement or stress-reduction claims.
- Meta: no body-part close-cropping, no implied negative self-perception, no personal-attribute
  targeting language ("as a new mum, you…"), no unverifiable superlatives.
- Email: physical postal address and a working one-click unsubscribe in every send. Consent-based
  lists only — never a purchased or scraped list. Honour unsubscribes within 24h.
- All AI-generated people in creative are fictional. Never generate a likeness of a real,
  identifiable person, and never imply a real person endorses CAVÁ.

## 8. Age policy — non-negotiable

The stated audience is 14–40. It is split:

- **Paid media (Meta, Google, any platform): minimum age 18.** Every ad set ships `age_min: 18`.
  Platform policy heavily restricts advertising to minors — interest, behaviour and lookalike
  targeting are unavailable for under-18 audiences in most markets, and swimwear or bare-skin
  creative may not be delivered to them at all. There is no version of this the agent overrides.
- **Email: 18+ at signup.** No knowingly collecting data from under-16s (GDPR Art. 8).
- **Organic social: reaches 14–17 and that is fine.** Content served to that audience is
  fully-covered styling only — robe closed, belt tied. No swimwear, no open-robe, no bare-torso
  frames in organic content or on any surface a teen audience sees.
- Models in all creative read as clearly adult. Never generate or brief a model who reads as
  under 18, in any context.

Swimwear and open-robe frames (e.g. the poolside reference) are **18+ paid placements only**,
and never the primary asset in a set.
